Homey's own Delay starts at one whole second. Advanced Waiting adds the missing piece: a Wait card that pauses a Flow for a number of milliseconds or seconds and then lets it continue in place, so the next card simply runs after it. Use it in the And column of a standard Flow, or anywhere in an Advanced Flow.

Homey stops a Flow that has been waiting for about ninety seconds, so this card accepts at most 85 seconds and explains why when you ask for more, instead of letting the Flow break silently. For longer pauses, use Homey's built-in Delay.
